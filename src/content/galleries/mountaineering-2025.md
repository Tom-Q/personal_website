These pictures are from several trips in 2025: a week at the Jervis Rifugio in Val Pellice for ice climbing and skimo, two weeks in Chamonix culminating in the Forbes Arête on the Chardonnet (in the cloud), and two short stays in the Écrins including climbing the Aiguille Dibona and the Pic Coolidge. Good times with good friends!
