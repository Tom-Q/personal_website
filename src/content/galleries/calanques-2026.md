A short trip to Marseille. We did a few multipitch routes, including the very memorable *Prends-moi sec au dessus du lagon bleu* (with waves and light coming inside the well from underneath the cliff), and what is probably the best route I have ever climbed: *La Promesse des Profondeurs*, with its start at sea level and six incredible pitches to the top.
